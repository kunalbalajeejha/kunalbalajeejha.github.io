easy Radio Support

easy Radio is a simple internet-radio player for iPhone: add the stations you like, tune between them with a dial, save songs you love and fall asleep with a timer.

Adding a station

In the Stations tab, tap Paste a livestream URL (or +). Paste a direct stream link (for example ending in .aac, .mp3 or .m3u8) or choose a .m3u / .pls playlist file. The app finds the stream and suggests a name and frequency. Many stations publish their stream link on their website ("listen in other players").

A station does not play

  • Check the link plays in Safari. Only https links are supported.
  • Some stations change their stream address; add the new link again.
  • Turn on Reconnect if the stream drops in Settings.
