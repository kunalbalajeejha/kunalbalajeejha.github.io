easy Radio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

easy Radio is an internet-radio player for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: the developer does not collect any data from the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party software development kits. The developer does not run any server for the app and receives no information from it.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The stations you add (name, frequency, colour and stream address).
  • Songs you save as favourites (title, artist, station name and the time you saved them).
  • Your settings (theme, sleep timer, reconnect and song-search choices).

This information is stored only on your device, inside the app. It is included in your device backups in the same way as other app data. You can delete any station or favourite in the app, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Radio streams you choose

When you play a station, your iPhone connects directly to the stream address you added. Like any internet connection, the server of that radio station or streaming provider receives your IP address and basic technical information (for example the app and system version). Those servers are run by the stations or their providers, not by the developer, and their own privacy policies apply.

Clipboard and other apps

The app reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste to add a station. When you search a song in another app, easy Radio opens that app or the Apple Music website with the song name, and may copy "Artist - Title" to the clipboard so you can paste it. Nothing else is shared.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
